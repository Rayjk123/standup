# Frost Watch roadmap to the 2027 bloom

The 2026 season is over for the early varieties. Everything below is judged by one question: will it be in place
before the first cold night of 2027, which in this valley can come in the second half of July.

## Must be ready by the first cold night

- **Night rosters** instead of the one shared alert list (see the night roster product intent).
- **Voice calls first** for a machine that will not start or a controller that has gone silent.
- **Weak inversion check** in the controller firmware, with its own alert.
- **Antenna masts and a repeater** for the three lower-hollow blocks, from the radio coverage walk.
- A pre-season checklist per machine: fuel solenoid, battery, relay test from the night map.

## Worth doing if there is time

- A season summary per grower: nights protected, machine hours, fuel, coldest minute per block.
- A warning when a probe's battery will not last the next cold spell.
- Showing the forecast minimum on the night map next to the live readings.

## Not planned

- Recognising the bud stage from photos. Growers set the stage; the agronomist checks it.
- Controlling the overhead sprinklers on the two cherry blocks. They stay manual.
- Offering Frost Watch to other co-ops.
