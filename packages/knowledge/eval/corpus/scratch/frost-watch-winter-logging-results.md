# Frost Watch winter logging results

Over six weeks of winter we left probes logging to SD cards in three blocks: four at blossom height and one at 10 m
on each wind machine tower. Nothing was sent anywhere.

## What the logs say

- On clear, still nights the lower-hollow blocks were 1.5 to 2.5 °C colder at blossom height than the blocks on the
  slope, and they cooled fastest in the two hours before dawn.
- The tower was warmer than the canopy on 19 of 23 cold nights. On the other four, all windy or cloudy, there was no
  inversion and a wind machine would not have helped.
- Wet-bulb was up to 1.8 °C below air temperature on dry nights, which is why the start rule should use wet-bulb.
- One of the twelve probes read 0.7 °C warm all winter; an ice bath check found it.

## What it means for the design

Use wet-bulb for starting, watch the tower probe to know whether a machine will help, and calibrate every probe
before the season.
