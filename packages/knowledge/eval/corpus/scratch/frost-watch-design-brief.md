# Frost Watch design brief

## Why

On a frost night the co-op's growers take turns driving round the blocks with a handheld thermometer and start each
wind machine by hand. A block that cools faster than expected loses its blossom before anyone gets there, and the
person driving gets no sleep at all.

## What it should do

- Know every block's temperature at blossom height all night, without anyone in the orchard.
- Start wind machines in time, and stop them once the danger has passed, so fuel is not burnt for nothing.
- Wake a person only when a person is needed.

## What it should not do

- Replace the growers' judgement about other protection (sprinklers, covers).
- Need anything installed on growers' phones beyond a web page.

## How we will know

- No block falls below its critical temperature with a working machine left off.
- Fewer machine hours than a season of hand starting.
- The night lead sleeps on quiet nights.
